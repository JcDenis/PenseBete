Things to do for this blog, shared by its users having the permission "manage todo lists". Nothing is shown on the public side.

Lists
A list is a single word, created with its first todo and gone with its last one.
Status
The button at the end of a line marks the todo as done, or to do again. Done todos are kept: show them with the filter "Status".
Selected todos
Mark them as done or to do, move them to another list, or delete them for good.
Dashboard
In your preferences, the favorite "Todo lists" gives the number of todos to do, and a dashboard option displays the last todos added.
